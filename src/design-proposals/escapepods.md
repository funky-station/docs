## I. Summary

This document intends to replace the manual evacuation shuttle with a station wide evacuation via escape pods to make the station feel more isolated.

## II. Problem

The current “issue” is that when evacuation is manually called a shuttle is dispatched from CentCom (might also be known as regional command) which then delivers the crew safe and sound to an outpost, this goes against one of the the target feelings funky is trying emulate that being isolation, I am of the personal opinion that if you know a shuttle can pick you up and get you to a safe and cozy outpost if things go horribly wrong you do not feel as isolated as you would otherwise.

A secondary and more minor problem is that calling the evacuation is seen as an easy way out, at this moment command calls evacuation quite easily without all too much argument and conflict.


## IV. Solution

Escape pods bid a good solution to both problems, instead of an evacuation shuttle being called (using the communication console’s call evacuation shuttle button) instead members of command would be authorizing the use of the station’s escape pods, which in practice is ordering the crew to abandon the station. With escape pods instead of an evacuation shuttle the decision to evacuate will feel a lot heftier and won’t just be the cozy “oh we’re going to end the shift a bit early” instead it will be “We’re abandoning this place” and the evacuation will feel more like an actual evacuation with people making their way to escape pods.

To go over the basics how these escape pods would work: When command authorizes the escape pods there would be a station wide announcement that command has authorized the use of the station’s escape pods after when a 10 minute countdown begins, after 5 minutes a point of no return is reached whereafter command cannot unauthorize the escape pods being used (like evac’s cancel window currently) and after another five minutes a station wide announcement would tell the crew that the escape pod’s launch mechanism are now unlocked and ready for launch, during this time members of command can use a escape pod control panel (in the escape pod itself) to launch the escape pod early, this period would last for about 5 minutes after which all escape pods are automatically launched, after this the escape pods land on some deserted planet (if arrivals is being retired could use that planet for this) and the round ends and the end of round timer begins.

Now to start, why this early launch by command members? Because it creates more conflict between the crew and command, imagine someone running towards an escape pod with the CMO and you in it chased by zombies and the CMO launches the escape pod early to not get caught by said zombies, you are now in a escape pod with a murderer (It’d probably be the best if when launching the escape pod first closed and bolted it’s doors and then after a few seconds launched so the people left behind can stare in the eyes of their killer) scenarios like these would be made possible by this early launch system.

It’d probably also be good to add quite a few filler announcements reminding people of the escape pods being authorized and to abandon ship.

(tiny note, it would be quite cool if the entire station shook a bit after an escape pod launched using RMC’s ported explosion system to give it more oomph)

## V. Considerations

Technical wise this would be quite some work to say the least and I am personally not knowledgeable enough to say how much it would take. Admin wise it probably wouldn’t cause any problems except for EORG rules which would have to be slightly amended but those are being reworked for forky if I heard correctly. Player wise this would mean a lot of news things, they no longer escape to CC but get stranded on some deserted planet and evacing would feel a lot more impactful.
