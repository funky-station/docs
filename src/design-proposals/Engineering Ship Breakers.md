## Overview
## I. Summary

This document proposes an addition to the engineering departments duties in the form of ship breaking for material acquisition. 

## II. Problem

As it stands Engineering primarily only constructs things, very rarely do they have a reason to engage with the deconstruction mechanics present, that would be common in their work. Additionally a majority of their work is purely reactionary and very little consistent tasks are present for Engineering to engage with. 

## III. Intent

If this is implemented I would hope it would give engineering a quaternary purpose in material gathering and a consistent thing to do throughout the round outside of the occasional engine maintenance or reactionary repairing.

I also would hope to see conflict erupting between departments, such as cargo over materials. Perhaps Engineering is being mistreated and cuts off a supply of materials to cargo. Which would force cargo to relent, or go to security who would have to pressure engineering or do more arrests for prisoners to mine 

This document is made in mind with cargo receiving new tasks so as to not overstep or remove them from the material logistics entirely. Such as material processing or good making.  


## IV. Solution

**Required Additions**

The following additions would be necessary to implement shipbreaking as a consistent activity for Engineering.

1. Salvage Magnet

A new or modified salvage magnet that allows Engineering to pull derelict ships into a designated area. Players would be able to select from several categories of ships, such as freighters, private vessels, and the occasional military vessel.

2. Dedicated Shipbreaking Area

A designated area of the station exposed to space, with enough room to accommodate ships of varying sizes. This area would also contain the necessary machinery and equipment to dismantle ships and process the resulting materials.

3. Variety of Salvageable Ships

A selection of ship designs with different layouts, materials, and potential hazards. These could include radiation leaks, primed thrusters, automated turrets, hostile fauna, and malfunctioning machinery. Different ship types would present different challenges and rewards. 

4. Material Processing Machinery

A furnace, deconstructor, or similar machine capable of breaking down salvaged objects and ship components into usable raw materials.

**Proposed Gameplay Loop**

These additions would establish a repeatable gameplay loop consisting of five stages:

Ship Retrieval: Engineering uses the salvage magnet to select and pull a derelict ship into the designated shipbreaking area.

Hazard Assessment: Engineers inspect the ship for potential dangers, such as active turrets, radiation, hostile fauna, or faulty machinery, and take the necessary precautions.

Dismantling: Once the ship is considered safe, Engineering begins dismantling its structure and recovering valuable components.

Material Processing: Recovered components are transported to the deconstructor or furnace and broken down into usable materials.

Material Distribution: The recovered materials are delivered to Cargo for storage and distribution to other departments as needed.
## V. Considerations



## Roundflow & Player interaction

Some engineers would be expected to be doing shipbreaking to gather the station materials. Every Ship would have various dangers ranging from minor to major, failure to handle them correctly would result in death, damage or destruction of things within the vicinity of the ship.

Engineers would be expected to take the materials to cargo for passing out, but some may take cuts of the materials which is intentional. If found out they would be punished by Security or the Chief Engineer.


## Administrative & Server Rule Impact (if applicable)


Some players may intentionally mess with the pulled in ships to cause death and or destruction in the imminent vicinity. Which without proper character reasons require admin intervention.  


# Technical Considerations

Would require a new machine large enough to intake multiple tile sized or bigger machines and objects.
