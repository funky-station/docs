## I. Summary

This document intends to propose a way to make of activating the station’s nuke (scuttle procedure) by the crew without need for admin intervention, it will do so by suggesting that the nuke codes are removed in favor for a key (nuclear authorization key) the Executive officer (XO) will have.


## II. Problem

Currently the nuclear fission explosive also known as the nuke (on forky) cannot be activated (unless nuclear operatives are added) during a round without admin intervention and forky is trying to minimize admin intervention during rounds unless an OOC rulebreak occurs.

From an in universe standpoint: If the captain wants to request the nuclear codes from Central it would take at absolute minimum 20 minutes in which time the station could already be long lost and posing a threat to the entire sector.

## III. Intent

If this is implemented I hope it will give the XO an even more important feel and solidify it as the station’s second in command, It would also help solidify the captain being the ultimate authority on the station.

If this were to be implemented hopefully it would create scenarios in which after the station is so overrun and a catastrophe the captain together with the XO will consider using the nuclear failsafe device, obviously the existence of this option alone would create much more tension with everyone as they will know that the captain and XO have the ability to scuttle the station if they deem it necessary and tension between all three groups is something we want.


## IV. Solution

The nuclear codes would be removed and replaced with a new item for the Executive Officer: The Nuclear Authorization Key. This would be a 1x2 item that just looks like a small blank keycard with a chain around it allowed it to be worn on the neck slot, when inserted into the nuclear fission device in combination with the captain’s Nuclear Authentication Disk it would unlock the arm option, this would make it a sort of two man system. 

This would absolutely need a bit of added SOP for XO and the captain about this which might include something that they both need to agree on it, but I’ll leave that to the SOP workgroup after I bring this to them.

## V. Considerations


### Technical: 
On the technical side of things the nuke would have to be pretty much entirely overhauled to remove the number keypad and make it require both the nuclear disk and nuclear key are needed for arming it.
And of course the Nuclear Key would have to be added.

### Admin:
This is intended to remove some admin intervention however it will also (most likely) cause a few new rulebreaks that most likely being play your character for when people arm the nuke when they really shouldn’t be, wether the addition of this feature is worth the risk of these extra rulebreaks is something the admins should talk about. And this change may need an addition to the play your character rule about detonating the nuke as the captain. 

### Players:
Players are already trusted in forky with quite a lot, being able to kill others, cause revolts etc, I personally do not think this would be that big of a leap of trust and from the culture and players I do think this is something players can be trusted with.
